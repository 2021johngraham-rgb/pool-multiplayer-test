    Play Texas Hold'em against three opponents, from the first deal to the final showdown.

    2,000 starting chips · No-limit betting · Play chips only

    A seat at the table

    1. You receive two private cards. Five shared cards appear across the flop, turn, and river.
    2. Make your best five-card hand using any combination of private and shared cards.
    3. Check when no bet is owed. Call to match a bet, raise to increase it, or fold to leave the hand.
    4. The last player remaining wins, or the strongest hand wins at showdown. Ties split the pot.
    5. All-ins can create side pots. The dealer button and blinds move after every hand.
